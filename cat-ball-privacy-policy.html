Cat Ball Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026 · Pozware

Cat Ball, made by Pozware, does not collect any personal information itself. Your game progress stays on your own device. The Android app shows family-friendly ads from Google AdMob, and this policy explains exactly what that involves.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves only what it needs to remember your game between sessions, and only on your device. Pozware never receives any of it.

What is savedWhy
Which rooms you have unlocked, your stars and best timesSo you can carry on and replay rooms
The cat you last pickedSo it is ready next time
High scores, with the room, cat and dateTo show your records on the High Scores screen
The name you type for the high score table (up to 10 letters)To label your scores; you choose what to type
Your Bubble Bonus recordTo show your best bubble count
Sound on or offSo your choice is remembered
A count of rooms finished since the last adSo an ad only appears after every third room you finish

None of this is linked to your real name, email address, device ID or location, and none of it is sent to the ad service.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app is free and is supported by full-screen ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear only between rooms, never while you are playing: after a room where the time runs out, and after every third room you finish. The web browser version has no ads.

Because Cat Ball is made for all ages, including children, every ad is set up under Google Play's Families policy:

  • Every ad request is tagged as directed to children, so Google treats every player as a child.
  • No personalised or interest-based ads and no remarketing. Ads are not chosen based on who you are or what you do in other apps.
  • Ads are limited to content rated G (general audiences).
  • The advertising ID is switched off. The app removes the permission that would let it read your device's advertising ID, so it is never sent.
  • Only Google AdMob is used, which is certified for Google Play's Families program. No other ad networks are used.

What the ad service receives

To deliver an ad, show it correctly, count it and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads software in the app sends some technical information to Google when an ad is requested or shown:

InformationWhat it is used for
IP addressDelivering the ad to your device and estimating a general region (country or city level), for example to show ads in the right language. Not precise location.
Interactions with adsWhether an ad was shown, closed or tapped, and app launches, so ads can be counted and fraud prevented
Diagnostic informationHow well the ad software performs, such as load times and crashes
App and device detailsSuch as device model, Android version, language and an app-specific identifier (app set ID), used for frequency capping, reporting and fraud prevention. Not the advertising ID.

This information is encrypted while it is sent. Google handles it under its own policies, including How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Pozware does not receive this information. AdMob only gives Pozware summary reports, such as how many ads were shown and how much they earned, which do not identify anyone.

Playing without ads: if the phone is offline, no ads are requested or shown and nothing is sent. The game works fully offline.

What we never collect

  • No accounts or sign-in: you never give us a name, email address or password.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking by Pozware.
  • No advertising ID, no personalised ads and no precise location.
  • No in-app purchases or payment details.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The Android app asks only for internet and network-status access, which are used to load ads and need no approval.

Pozware never sells or shares your information.

Children

Cat Ball is suitable for all ages and takes part in Google Play's Families program. Pozware collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are tagged as child-directed so that Google limits the data it uses in line with children's privacy laws such as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and no personalised ads are shown. A name typed for the high score table stays on the device and is never sent anywhere. Parents with questions can contact us using the details below.

App stores and platforms
