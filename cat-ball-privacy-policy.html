Cat Ball Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026 · Pozware

Cat Ball, made by Pozware, does not collect, send or share any personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your own device.

What the game stores

The game saves only what it needs to remember your game between sessions, and only on your device.

What is savedWhy
Which rooms you have unlocked, your stars and best timesSo you can carry on and replay rooms
The cat you last pickedSo it is ready next time
High scores, with the room, cat and dateTo show your records on the High Scores screen
The name you type for the high score table (up to 10 letters)To label your scores; you choose what to type
Your Bubble Bonus recordTo show your best bubble count
Sound on or offSo your choice is remembered

None of this is linked to your real name, email address, device ID or location.

Where it is kept and how to delete it

You can erase everything the game has saved at any time.

VersionWhere your data is keptHow to delete it
AndroidThe app's private storage on your phone; Android may include it in your own device backupSettings → Apps → Cat Ball → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall
Web browserYour browser's storage for the game's pageClear site data for the page in your browser settings

What we never collect

  • No accounts or sign-in: you never give us a name, email address or password.
  • No internet connection: the game works fully offline and never sends anything to Pozware or anyone else. The Android app does not even request internet access.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No in-app purchases or payment details.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The Android app asks for no permissions at all.

Because nothing leaves your device, Pozware never sees, sells or shares your information.

Children

Cat Ball is suitable for all ages. It collects no information from anyone, including children, so no parental consent is needed to play. A name typed for the high score table stays on the device and is never sent anywhere.

App stores and platforms

The store or platform you get the game from runs its own services, such as downloads, updates and optional diagnostics. They handle that information under their own privacy policies, not this one:

  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play and Android)

Google Play may give Pozware summary statistics, such as install counts, that do not identify you.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what it stores or starts connecting to the internet, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
