Privacy Policy

Ninja Cats - Dungeon Saga and Ninja Cats - Free Trial
Published by Pozware · Effective 17 November 2026

In short: Ninja Cats shows ads. The ads are supplied by Google AdMob, which collects some information from your device in order to deliver them. Pozware itself does not collect, store or sell your personal information. The game has no accounts and no in-app purchases, and your game progress stays on your own device.

1. Who we are

Ninja Cats - Dungeon Saga (the "game") is made and published by Pozware ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Android version on Google Play, the Windows version on the Microsoft Store, and the Free Trial versions of each.

2. Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

3. Advertising

The Android version of the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, an advertising service run by Google. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob's software inside the game may automatically collect and send to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier supplied by Android);
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (such as your country or city);
  • device information, such as model, operating system version, screen size, language and mobile network;
  • information about how you interact with ads and the game, such as which ads were shown or tapped, when the app was opened, and crash or performance data.

Google and its advertising partners use this information to show ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure how ads perform, and to detect fraud. Depending on your settings and where you live, it may also be used to show you ads based on your interests ("personalised ads").

This information goes directly from your device to Google. We do not receive anything that identifies you. We see only combined reports, such as how many ads were shown and the revenue they earned.

Google's handling of this information is described here:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

The Windows version of the game does not show ads.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Turn off personalised ads or reset your advertising ID: on Android, open Settings, then Google, then Ads (on some devices: Settings, Privacy, Ads). You can delete or reset your advertising ID there. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.
  • Manage Google's ad personalisation: visit adssettings.google.com.
  • Consent in Europe and the UK: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings.

5. Permissions

The Android version uses internet access and network state so that ads can load, the advertising ID so that ads can be delivered and measured, and vibration for a short buzz when a weapon fires (which you can turn off in the game). The game itself still plays without a connection; ads simply will not load.

6. Information saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your own device only:

  • your progress (which levels are unlocked and cleared, and your current run);
  • your high scores, badges and the three initials you enter for the high score table;
  • your chosen character and settings, such as sound and vibration.

We do not send this information anywhere and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

If you have turned on your device's own backup feature (for example Google's Android backup), your device maker or platform provider may include the game's saved data in your personal device backup. That backup is controlled by you and by that provider under their privacy policy, not by us.

7. Other third parties

Apart from Google AdMob, the game contains no other analytics, tracking or social media software. We do not sell personal information.

The store you download the game from (Google Play or the Microsoft Store) may collect information about your download, device and purchases under its own privacy policy. We receive only the anonymous, combined statistics those stores provide to all developers, such as the number of installs.

8. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where the game may be played by children, ads are requested from Google with child-directed settings, which means personalised ads are turned off and only ads rated suitable for a general audience are requested. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child's use of the game, please contact us at the address below.

9. Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases. If you buy the game, the payment is handled entirely by the store. We never see or store your payment details.

10. How long information is kept

We hold no personal information about players, so we have nothing to keep or delete. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies, linked above. To ask Google about it, use the tools in your Google account or the links in section 3.

11. Contacting us

If you email us with a question or a problem, we will see your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use that only to reply to you and to fix the issue, and we do not add it to any mailing list.

12. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top before the change is released.
